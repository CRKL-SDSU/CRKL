Meet The Team!

Ryan

[Backend Developer]

I believe that this project is unique because it tries to tackle the challenge of acquiring and organizing mass amounts of data into one localized space. We felt this challenge was necessary because astronomy is a very important research field whose barrier to entry is the sheer volume of complex information. As a result, not many people can find a proper introduction to the field, whether due to educational, financial, or personal circumstances, and we hope to provide a free to use, efficient, and helpful website that will remove a lot of that astronomical pressure (heh). We at CRKL-Space want to help aspiring astronomers and researchers remember the history of the universe and the shape the stars were given.

Kazuhiro

[Database Developer]

There is a lot of material on space exploration all around, but it is hardly collected together in one place. CRKL Space brings all of this together in one single, searchable mirror catalog. For this project, I have specified the API specs and MySQL schema and implemented backend API endpoints for information on missions, space agencies, spacecraft, and launches. CRKL Space is relationship-oriented, not just a search tool. The entities are linked to their related entities with normalized intermediate tables. This means that when you search for "Artemis," you'll see related agencies and launch information and be able to navigate between records and follow the full scope of the mission as if you were exploring the universe of information. For me, CRLK-space is a great opportunity to demonstrate how raw data can be transformed into something that people can actually explore through careful database design.

Lia

[Team Lead/Frontend Developer]

CRLK Space aims to create an environment that makes space-related information accessible, organized, and easy to understand. Our main goal is to eliminate the tedious task of sifting through copious amounts of information scattered across the web. What makes CRLK Space unique is its focus on simplicity and efficiency. Current websites that provide space-related information can often be filled with dense text and lengthy articles. Instead, CRLK Space provides users with the essential information they need: Name, Company, Date, Description, and Image. Our goal is to make learning and researching space more accessible by presenting important information in a simple, clear, and easy-to-navigate format.
