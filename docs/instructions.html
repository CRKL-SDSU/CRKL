Instructions

How to run CRKL-Space and explore its catalog of missions, agencies, spacecraft, and launches

You use CRKL-Space through its Explore page (/explore), where you search the catalog and open records to see how they are connected.

Run CRKL-Space

CRKL-Space runs on your own computer with Docker. Follow Executable: run it locally on the Source Code page for the requirements, the configuration file, and the start command, then open http://localhost:3000/explore.

What's in the catalog

After you load the real data, the catalog holds missions, agencies, spacecraft, and launches from Launch Library 2. Each record shows its source at the bottom: Source: Launch Library 2 for real data, or Source: Dummy for the fictional sample data used by our tests.

ResourceWhat it describesExample
MissionsA space mission and its descriptionApollo 11, Gemini IV (Gemini 4)
AgenciesA space agency or company, with its abbreviation and countryNational Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA), European Space Agency (ESA)
SpacecraftA spacecraft and its typeApollo Columbia CSM-107 (Capsule)
LaunchesA launch with its date, window, status, and launch providerSaturn V | Apollo 11

Missions are the center of the catalog. Each mission can be linked to several agencies, spacecraft, and launches, and agencies, spacecraft, and launches are connected to each other through missions. Each launch also names its launch provider, the agency that carried it out.

Note: Relationships are loaded as Launch Library 2 records them. It lists the agencies behind only a few missions, and the data we load doesn't say which spacecraft flew on a launch, so most real agencies and spacecraft have few or no related missions. Launches are linked to their missions.

Explore: searching the catalog

Open http://localhost:3000/explore.

Choose what to search

Under the search box, select Search in: Missions, Agencies, Spacecraft, or Launches. The results always show records of this type. Switching type clears the current results.

Type a keyword and press Search

  • Search is case-insensitive and matches anywhere inside a field. gemini finds Gemini IV (Gemini 4).
  • Leave the box empty and press Search to list records of the selected type.

The search box accepts Gmail-style queries:

QueryMatches
apollo lunarrecords matching both words (AND is the default)
apollo OR geminieither word (OR must be uppercase)
apollo -soyuzrecords that do not match the word
"saturn v"the exact phrase
tag:moonmissions with that tag; other types match through their missions (see Tags)
(apollo OR gemini) -testparentheses group terms

Fields searched for each type:

Searching inMatches on
Missionsname, description, target body, status, source, source ID
Agenciesname, abbreviation, country code, source, source ID
Spacecraftname, spacecraft type, source, source ID
Launchesname, status, source, source ID

Include related records (on by default)

When Include related records is checked, a record also matches if a linked record matches your keyword:

Searching inAlso matches when…
Missionsa linked agency, spacecraft, or launch matches
Agencies / Spacecraft / Launchesa linked mission matches (name, description, target body, status)

Examples with the real data:

Search inKeywordRelated onRelated off
LauncheslunarAlso launches whose mission description mentions "lunar"Only the few launches with "lunar" in their own fields
Missions"saturn v"Missions flown on a Saturn V launch, such as Apollo 4 and Apollo 8Only missions that mention "Saturn V" in their own text
AgenciesapolloNASA, through its Apollo missionsNo results: no agency mentions Apollo in its own fields

Uncheck the box for exact, single-table matching.

Read the results

The heading shows how many results are displayed and how many records matched in total. Each result card shows:

  • a thumbnail image, or an icon for the record type when there is no image
  • the record type and name
  • a few key fields, such as a launch's date, status, and launch provider; empty values show as —
  • a mission's tags
  • the source and source ID, in small type

Note: Explore displays up to 100 results per search. If more records match, use a more specific query.

Explore: viewing details

Click any result card to open its detail panel.

The panel shows:

  1. The image, with its credit and license when Launch Library 2 provides them.
  2. The record's fields, the same list for every record of a type. Empty values are shown as —.
  3. Related records, grouped by type. A mission shows its agencies, spacecraft, and launches. An agency, spacecraft, or launch shows its missions.
  4. For a mission, its tags, where you can add or remove tags.
  5. At the bottom, the source and when the record was added and updated, and a small copy icon. It copies a prompt for an AI assistant with the record's details and links to its Launch Library 2 data, for finding primary sources and more detail.

Following links

Click any related record to open it in the same panel. This lets you walk through the catalog, e.g.:

National Aeronautics and Space Administration → Apollo 11 → Apollo LM | Apollo 11

Tags

Tags are labels anyone can add to a mission in its detail panel; there is no login yet. Tag names are stored in lowercase, can use letters, digits, spaces, and hyphens, and are at most 30 characters; a mission can have up to 10 tags. Click a tag on a result card or in a detail panel to search for it, or type tag:moon, or tag:"ocean worlds" for a tag with spaces.

Panel controls

ControlAction
← BackReturn to the previous record you viewed
Forward →Go forward again after using Back
×Close the panel
Click outside the panelClose the panel
Esc keyClose the panel

Back and Forward work like browser history. If you go back and then open a different record, the forward history is discarded. Closing the panel clears the history.
